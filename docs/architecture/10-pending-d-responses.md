## Operations and missions

**[X0] How missions are stored**
Suggestion: one running record for the whole operation, cut into mission chapters. Characters keep money, items and contacts with no copying.
- [ ] Agree
- [x] Change: according to notes ______
- [ ] Later
- Notes: Campaign > Operation > Mission > Session
- A Session is one unit of play between players. (1-3 hours avg.)
  A Mission is a scenario or objective that agents are trying to complete. It is accomplished across a series of sessions. (1-3 sessions avg.)
  An Operation is a long-horizon goal, story arc, or other organization/collection of Missions. (1-10 missions avg.) Each operation is managed by a handler. An operation can have multiple missions running concurrently but not simultaneously
  A Campaign is a series of Operations managed by a GM. A campaign can have multiple concurrent or simultaneous Operations
  Each of these units need to be archived.
  
- A GM may have more than one handler character/persona/alias for different operations. A player may have more than one alias in an operation or mission. An alias may appear in multiple missions. Ask any more questions that you need to expand on this.
