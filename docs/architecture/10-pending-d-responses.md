**How to fill in a block**

- Tick **one** box: `[x]`.
  - **Agree** means "use the suggestion as written".
  - **Change** means "do it, but differently": write what to do on the line below.
  - **No** means "don't do this": say why if you like.
  - **Later** means "not decided yet".
- **Notes** is for anything else: a page number, a wording, "ask me again after playtesting".
- Skip anything you don't want to answer now. A blank block counts as **Later**.
- The suggestion is never decided until a box is ticked.
